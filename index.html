FAQ

WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW

WHAT METALS DO YOU COMMONLY WORK WITH? +

We work with carbon steel, stainless steel, and aluminum.

IS CUSTOMIZATION AVAILABLE? +

Absolutely. Custom trailers and braais are our specialty. We will work with you to ensure your project meets your exact dimensions and aesthetic requirements.

DO YOU BUILD CUSTOM BRAAIS? +

Yes, we design and manufacture heavy-duty custom braais, including built-in units, mobile braais, and spit braais built to endure extreme heat.

ARE WELDING REPAIRS DONE ON-SITE? +

Yes, we offer mobile welding services for urgent structural repairs, heavy equipment, and gate fixes directly at your location.

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE? +

Project timelines vary depending on complexity. Small custom braais take 3-5 days, while larger trailers or structural framing can take 2-3 weeks.

DO YOU GUARANTEE YOUR WORKMANSHIP? +

We stand behind our craftsmanship with a solid 12-month structural warranty on all welding and metal fabrication work.
